Automation Watchdog

Last run: never

What it is for. As more of the newsletter runs on automations, the risk stops being "did someone forget" and becomes "did the automation quietly not run". The watchdog is the second pair of eyes on that, so a job that silently failed gets noticed the next morning rather than the week a sponsor asks where their report is.

What it does. Once a day it checks what should have happened across beehiiv, the CRM, Notion and Slack against what the automations actually did, in both directions: what should have fired and did not, and what fired that should not have.

What it never does. It never does the work itself and never writes to anything a sponsor can see. It only reports. Every run is logged below.
